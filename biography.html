About Dr. Yakubu Adamu

A Journey of Finance, Service and Leadership

Dr. Yakubu Adamu is a finance and management professional whose career has combined experience in the private banking sector and public service.

Before entering government, he spent several years working in Nigeria's financial services industry, holding positions in marketing, retail banking, business development and public-sector banking.

In 2023, he transitioned into public service when he was appointed Commissioner for Finance and Economic Development in Bauchi State.

Early Life & Background

Roots and Early Years

Dr. Yakubu Adamu was born on 12 August 1980 in Gwabba, Bauchi metropolis, Bauchi State, Nigeria.

He hails from Gulbun, Doguwa Ward, Giade Local Government Area of Bauchi State. His early life was shaped by experiences across Gwabba, Ilelah, where he began his primary education, and Karofin Madaki, where he later lived.

Public Service & Politics

Commissioner for Finance and Economic Development

Dr. Yakubu Adamu formally entered public office in 2023 when he was appointed Commissioner for the Bauchi State Ministry of Finance and Economic Development.

His professional background in banking, financial management and economic development formed an important part of his transition into public administration.

2023 – April 2026

Served as Commissioner for Finance and Economic Development in Bauchi State.
